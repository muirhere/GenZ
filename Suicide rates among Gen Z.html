The story

Gen Z was born 1997-2012, so the first members turn 10, the youngest age tracked for suicide data, in 2007. Rates among Gen Z changed over time and differ between male and other genders. No single cause explains these trends. Prevention, connection and support matter.

Data: the values in the rows array are illustrative sample numbers, not official statistics. Replace them with rates from CDC WONDER, Underlying Cause of Death (ICD-10 X60-X84), for people born 1997-2012. Regional values (nine US Census divisions, 2018-2022 pooled) are sample data too. Blank years before 2007 have no Gen Z data; dashed lines after 2022 are provisional. Small counts are suppressed and deaths may be undercounted. CDC WONDER records sex as male or female only, so the "other genders" series needs a source that reports gender beyond male, or a clearly stated definition.
